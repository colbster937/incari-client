# Code of conduct

Incari is made by volunteers. In the project's issues,
discussions, pull requests and other spaces, we ask everyone to:

- be respectful and patient, even when you disagree;
- assume good intent, and criticize work, not people;
- never harass, threaten, demean or dox anyone, or share their private information;
- never post or link builds of the game, cheats, or other people's paid content.

Maintainers may edit or remove anything that breaks these rules, and may warn or ban the person
who posted it, depending on how serious it was.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the post, or contact a maintainer (listed in
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS)) privately. Reports are kept confidential.

This code of conduct is loosely based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
